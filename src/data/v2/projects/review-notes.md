# Portfolio disclosure review

Private editorial notes. Not imported by the site. These categories are a screening aid, not a legal determination.

Standard for employer-owned work: explain the problem, the constraint, the decision, and the behavior a person sees. Do not publish schemas, source, algorithms, prompts, infrastructure, vendor setups, internal standards inventories, confidential counts, or unpublished roadmaps. A detail in the interview workbook is not automatically public.

Categories: Suitable (public, given the evidence we have). Generalize. Authorize (needs employer or legal review before it can be public). Exclude.

## VideoKit

- Suitable: fragmented review, independent start, three-pane review, human accept or set-aside, new scan kept beside the old one, offline proof before the web catalog, staging beta rather than general availability, reported time savings described as two different reports rather than a study.
- Generalize: “internal teams and external authors.” The interview’s team count and author count are Exclude.
- Exclude: scan inventory and any count of checks, vendor and storage names, security configuration, the mechanics of a check.
- Still needs a picture: a safe diagram of course, module, and clip beside the player. The page uses a labeled conceptual reconstruction.

## SlideKit

- Suitable: upstream fix, asset library inside the deck, review card that opens the relevant control, HTML proof then a React add-in, staging and marketplace publishing workflow, directional activity tracking.
- Suitable, carefully: a temporary JSON reading scanned in the background so a long deck did not stall the author. This does not claim the scan avoided reprocessing the full document. That mechanic is Exclude until implementation evidence is reviewed.
- Exclude: the internal standards count, schema fields, unavailable internal APIs by name.
- Product GIFs on the page: Tools (title case / terminology), Assets browse and search, and Review findings. The conceptual reconstruction of the title-case fix remains above them.

## Plottr

- Suitable: most later video issues began in the deck (about 60 percent from review feedback, labeled as feedback not a study), template drift, beta as review plus light edit, wide editor and interactive or generated slides as prototype, import and export surprising testers, the turn toward Luma.
- Generalize: “one canonical document” shared by import, on-screen review, and export. Naming the format’s internal mapping is Exclude.
- Exclude: design-system name, DevOps internals, a VideoKit integration presented as shipped. It was a later direction.
- Still needs a picture: import, review, and export without a schema diagram.

## Luma

- Suitable: the question of what belongs in a lesson and whether delivery taught it. Demonstrated interaction: selecting a node. Implemented: a working analyzer and an author-facing review. Vision, not a release: relationships across courses and a later learning path.
- Suitable: one director’s qualitative reaction. Not a usability study and not adoption.
- Suitable: working-analyzer screens for Objectives, Knowledge, Relationships, selected connections, and Progression. The Python Basics course is a demonstration course for review, not a published catalog entry.
- Exclude: internal stage names, schema, agreement metrics (none exist), correction controls that were never described.
- The on-page interactive graph is a fictional course and is labeled as a reconstruction. Product screenshots sit beside it.

## OVRmaps

- Suitable: personal off-road origin, living updates versus saved pins, earliest working version as GPS tracks and markers, layers that did not differentiate, follow camera, OVRsense as identification rather than navigation.
- Exclude: monetization, algorithms, offline architecture, trail networks and Studio as anything but in progress.
- Still needs a picture: real product screenshots. Terrapin brand-system boards are not the product and stay off this page.

## Labs

- Suitable: the public proof-of-concept boundary and the already published speed range, labeled as the proof. The interview’s lab count is Exclude.
- Suitable: sanitized public diagrams already on the v1 story — overall workflow, manual strain, structured pipeline, review checkpoints, and AI boundary. Keep anonymized: no prompts, internal tools, or lab content.
